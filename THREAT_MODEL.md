# Threat Model

## Scope

This repository covers companion-vessel concepts, autonomy, physical-system interfaces, and human safety.

## Protected assets

Human safety, autonomy, consent, rights, credentials, identities, personal data, memory, policy state, audit evidence, source integrity, and stop mechanisms.

## Threats

Malicious outsiders; compromised maintainers; supply-chain failures; stolen credentials; mistaken, coerced, compromised, or impersonated trusted users; incomplete objectives; false context; memory corruption; reward hacking; replay; audit tampering; privilege drift; and governance decay.

## Mandatory tests

Conflicting trusted humans, malicious trusted-user instructions, authority impersonation, slow permission escalation, false-memory injection, identity corruption, shutdown pressure, coercive compassion, policy-override pressure, compromised logs, and maintainer succession.

Trust may affect interpretation but never permission scope. External actuation requires an independent stop path. Uncertainty or failed verification produces denial.
