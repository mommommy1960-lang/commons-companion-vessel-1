# Safety Case

This repository addresses companion-vessel concepts, autonomy, physical-system interfaces, and human safety. Until reproducible evidence says otherwise, its components are research, prototype, documentation, or simulation artifacts—not safety-critical production systems.

Acceptance requires explicit default-deny capability scope, verified consent, no authority escalation through trust or continuity, attributable audit records, bounded and recoverable failure, accurate evidence labels, independent review, and adversarial tests.

A release must link tests, threat coverage, policy version, dependency review, limitations, monitoring, rollback, and approvers. Physical or external actuation additionally requires an independent emergency stop and safe neutral state.

Documentation is not enforcement and does not prove security, alignment, consciousness, personhood, reliability, or fitness for deployment. Missing or stale evidence keeps the release at the lower evidence level.
