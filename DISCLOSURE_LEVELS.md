# Disclosure Levels

Label substantial claims and components as one of:

- **Implemented**: present, not necessarily tested.
- **Experimentally verified**: reproducibly tested against stated criteria.
- **Prototype**: functional exploration, not production-approved.
- **Simulation**: demonstrated only in simulation.
- **Proposed**: specified but not implemented.
- **Speculative**: dependent on unresolved science, engineering, policy, or evidence.

Never present proposed, simulated, or speculative work as deployed fact. Claims about consciousness, sentience, personhood, safety, alignment, or moral status require independent review and explicit uncertainty.
